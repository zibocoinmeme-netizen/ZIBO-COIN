# ZIBO Community

## The community is part of ZIBO

The community is not an audience. The community is part of ZIBO.

Members are encouraged to:

- Create memes
- Remix ZIBO
- Create artwork
- Tell stories
- Create jokes
- Participate in lore
- Share ZIBO
- Build community culture

The goal is to make people feel that they are helping create ZIBO rather than simply watching a token project.

Prioritize character development, original memes, humor, lore, internet culture, community participation, and creative posts.

Avoid excessive financial messaging and do not manufacture hype through unsupported claims.
