# ZIBO Brand Guide

## Identity

**Name:** ZIBO COIN  
**Ticker:** $ZIBO  
**Character:** ZIBO  
**Motto:** JUST MEME IT.

## Personality

ZIBO should feel:

- Original
- Funny
- Internet-native
- Simple
- Memetic
- Positive
- Irreverent
- Creative
- Community-driven

ZIBO should never feel like a corporation pretending to be a meme.

## Voice

Short. Confident. Funny. Simple. Mysterious when appropriate. Community-oriented. Meme-friendly.

Avoid corporate language, artificial hype, fake partnerships, fake statistics, financial promises, and excessive crypto-financial messaging.

## Character

ZIBO is an original hybrid meme character with a dog/fox-like appearance:

- Orange furry body
- Dog/fox-like face
- Green mask or green facial area
- Large expressive eyes
- Black nose
- Friendly, mischievous smile

The core characteristics should remain recognizable across different environments, situations, costumes, cultures, and memes.

## Visual language

Common visual elements include green, orange, space, Earth, internet culture, digital environments, and meme aesthetics.

Do not redesign ZIBO into a generic dog or copy the identity, logo, character design, or branding of existing meme projects.

## Core phrases

> ZIBO IS ZIBO.

> JUST MEME IT.

> Same internet. Different vibe.

> GOOD IDEAS START AS MEMES.
