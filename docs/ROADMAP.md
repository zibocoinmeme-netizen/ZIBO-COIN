# ZIBO Roadmap

The roadmap is stage-based rather than date-driven. Progress is measured by project maturity, not artificial deadlines.

## STAGE 01 — CHARACTER

Establish ZIBO's recognizable visual identity.

## STAGE 02 — MEMES

Create consistent, original ZIBO content.

## STAGE 03 — CULTURE

Develop ZIBO's language, lore, and community identity.

## STAGE 04 — COMMUNITY

Grow an organic community around ZIBO.

## STAGE 05 — MOVEMENT

Expand ZIBO beyond individual posts into recognizable internet culture.

## STAGE 06 — ZIBO COIN

Evaluate and prepare the token launch when sufficient resources and community support exist.

The roadmap does not promise exchange listings, market capitalization, price targets, profits, or financial performance.
