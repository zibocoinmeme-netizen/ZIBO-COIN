# ZIBO COIN Code of Conduct

ZIBO is community-driven. Keep the community creative, welcoming, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of other contributors.
- Welcome different creative approaches.
- Critique ideas without attacking people.
- Give credit where appropriate.
- Keep discussion focused on building ZIBO.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse
- Doxxing or publishing private information
- Spam, scams, impersonation, or deceptive financial claims
- Deliberately misleading project information
- Publishing secrets or credentials

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and repository.
